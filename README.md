## Case Study

**Organisation / scenario:** Ikojn, a simulated online store selling 100% Made-in-Kenya women's wear.

**Nature of the business:** Ikojn sells women's clothing to customers in Kenya through its website.

**Users of the database:** Store manager, customer service team, order processing team, logistics/delivery team, and customers (through the website).

**Major data stored:** Customers (name, phone number, delivery address), products (name, size, colour, price, stock quantity), categories, orders, order items (product and quantity), payments (amount paid, method, date), and deliveries.

**Key operations the database will support:**

1. Register a customer and store their delivery address.
2. Add and update products, prices and stock levels.
3. Browse and search products by category, size or colour.
4. Place an order with one or more products and quantities.
5. Reduce stock when an order is placed, and reject the order if stock is not enough.
6. Record a payment for an order, for example M-Pesa or card.
7. Update delivery status (processing, dispatched, delivered).
8. Generate reports: sales per product, orders per customer, and low-stock items.
